Privacy policy

Your listening is yours

Poddeck is a podcast app for iPhone. It has no account, no tracking, no analytics and no adverts, and it doesn't sell or share anything about you. This page sets out exactly what goes where.

Last updated 15 November 2026.

The short version

  • No sign-up and no Poddeck account. Poddeck has no servers of its own that hold your data.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • What you follow, your Up Next, your progress and your settings stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.
  • Transcripts, ad detection and chapter titles are made on your iPhone. Audio is never sent anywhere to be processed.
  • Nothing about you is sold or shared with anyone.

On your iPhone

Downloaded episodes, transcripts, listening history, stats, recent searches and transcription timings are kept on your iPhone. They are part of your iPhone's normal iCloud Backup if you use it (downloaded audio is left out of the backup to save space). Deleting the app deletes them.

Transcribing an episode, finding ad breaks and making chapters all happen on the iPhone itself, using Apple's built-in speech recognition, sound analysis and, where your iPhone supports it, Apple's on-device language model.

In your own iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, the shows you follow and their settings, Up Next, what you've played and how far you got, ad breaks and corrections, chapters, bookmarks and clips, and the app's settings are synced to your own private iCloud database and iCloud key-value storage. That way a new iPhone or a reinstall gets them back.

That data lives in your Apple account under Apple's iCloud terms. The app's developer cannot see or read it. You can turn iCloud off for Poddeck in the iPhone's Settings at any time.

What the app fetches from the internet

  • Podcast feeds, audio, artwork, transcripts and chapters come straight from each show's own host, as with any podcast app. Those hosts see an ordinary request from your iPhone (including its internet address), under their own privacy policies.
  • Search and Discover use Apple's public podcast directory (the iTunes Search API) and Apple's public chart feeds. Apple receives the words you search for and the charts you open, as it would in Apple Podcasts. Opening an Apple Podcasts link you paste is looked up the same way.
  • Private subscriber feeds (Patreon, Supercast and the like) are fetched straight from that service with the link you pasted. The link stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

None of these requests include an identifier for you from Poddeck, because there isn't one.

New-episode alerts

For everyone, alerts come from the app checking your shows in the background and showing a notification on the iPhone itself. Nothing leaves your phone for this.

The app's owner also runs an optional instant-alerts job (an open-source script on GitHub). It applies only to the owner's own iPhone: the app on any other iPhone doesn't send it anything and doesn't receive its alerts. For the owner, the list of followed shows is sealed (encrypted) on the phone so that only that job can open it, and the job keeps only fingerprints of the feeds and episodes it has seen, not their titles or links. Its logs never print feed links or titles.

Sharing

When you share a clip, a quote or a link, it goes only where you choose to send it through the iPhone's share sheet. Exporting your shows as an OPML file works the same way.

Children

Poddeck doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions about privacy are welcome: see the support page.
