Poddeck

A clickable prototype of the deck design. Everything on screen does something.
  • Swipe the top card left when heard. Swipe right a little: it comes back after the next one. All the way: to the bottom
  • Tap the stack to fan it out; pull down to close it; drag a card by its handle
  • Tap the artwork for the player: Options, Read along, Chapters, AirPlay
  • Shows: flick the show deck sideways
  • Find: search, charts and Follow
  • Gear on Up next: settings, Layout, stats
Made-up shows and colour-block artwork. Nothing is saved: reload to start again.
