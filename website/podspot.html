Skip ads

A Skip ad button, right when you need it.

While you listen, Podspot quietly checks the next few minutes of the episode for sponsor reads, promo codes and "after the break". When an ad starts, a Skip ad button appears in the player and the mini player and jumps you to where the show picks up again.

  • Breaks are marked in gold on the scrubber.
  • Wrong call? Tap Not an ad and it's remembered.
  • Prefer hands-free? Turn on skipping automatically.
  • Ads are found in your own download, so inserted ads that change from listener to listener are caught too.

Transcripts

Read along, search everything, quote anything.

Podspot uses the show's own transcript when there is one. For everything else, tap Transcribe now and your iPhone writes one, telling speakers apart and marking music and ad breaks. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  • Lines grouped every 20 seconds or so, and at each pause.
  • Follows playback. Tap any line to play from there.
  • Name the speakers once; the names stick for that show.
  • Search across every transcript and jump straight to the moment.
  • Transcribe only when you ask, or automatically while charging.

The player

Coloured by the artwork. Tuned for voices.

The full player takes its colour from each episode's artwork, with text kept easy to read. Underneath are the controls podcast listeners actually use.

  • Speed from 1.0× to 2.0×, with voices kept natural.
  • Trim silences speeds through pauses; Boost voices evens out quiet and loud speakers.
  • Skip back and forward set separately (5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 seconds). Touch and hold a skip button to change it on the spot.
  • Sleep timer, from 5 minutes to the end of the episode.
  • Chapters from the show, or made automatically from the transcript.
  • Play on AirPods, speakers, your TV or the car, with "Playing on …" so you know where the sound is going.

The rest of what's inside.

Downloads that tidy up

New episodes from your auto-add shows download in the background, on Wi-Fi only if you like. Finished episodes are deleted straight away, or kept for 24 hours.

Keep playing

When Up Next runs out, Podspot carries on with this week's new episodes from your shows, in the same order the queue would use.

Alerts that say where

Turn on the bell for a show and the alert tells you where the new episode went: "Added to the top of Up Next".

Discover

Search Apple's podcast directory, browse by category, see the UK top charts and get "Because you listen to…" suggestions.

Clips and bookmarks

Share a 5 to 30 second clip as a quote card, with the audio, a link to the moment, or as a short video. Every clip is saved to My clips.

Tidy back catalogues

Following a show with 500 episodes? Select all and mark them played, and only new ones will show as unplayed.

Library

New episodes as big cards in each show's colours, with a play button showing the length, and all your shows in a grid.

Search

Find a show, an episode or a line someone said, then play from that exact moment or add it to Up Next.

Bring your shows

Search by name, or paste a link from Apple Podcasts or a show's RSS feed.

Private by design

No account. No Podspot server. No tracking.

Transcripts, ad finding, speaker labels and chapter titles are all made on your iPhone. Podspot talks only to the shows you follow and Apple's free podcast directory. Speaker labels download a small model once, the first time you use them.
