What it costs

Everything explained. Everything switchable.

01 · Up Next

A queue that keeps every show in order

You decide where each show's new episodes go. Poddeck promises one thing: a show's episodes are always played oldest first, wherever they land.

How it works
  1. Set each show to Top, Bottom or Off. Newly followed shows start on Bottom.
  2. Rank your Top shows. A new episode from a Top show goes right after that show's last queued episode, or, if it has none queued, above every lower-ranked show. Your favourite stays first.
  3. Bottom shows go to the end. Off shows wait quietly on their own page.
  4. Play next, Play last and drag to reorder still work. "Keep each show in order" stops you breaking a show's order by accident (you can turn it off).

    02 · Transcripts

    Read along, search, jump in

    Every episode can have a transcript that follows playback. Tap any line to jump there, find words inside an episode, or search everything you've ever transcribed.

    How it works
    1. If the show publishes its own transcript, Poddeck uses it, with speaker names.
    2. If not, your iPhone writes one using Apple's built-in speech recognition, from the downloaded audio. The audio never leaves the phone.
    3. It only transcribes when you ask, unless you choose "While charging" or "As soon as downloaded". You can stop it any time.
    4. Music is spotted on the phone too and shown as its own row; ad breaks show as a dashed band.

    03 · Ad skipping

    Skip ads with one tap, or not at all

    When an ad break plays, a Skip ad button appears in the player and the mini player. It's your call every time, unless you tell Poddeck to skip on its own.

    How it works
    1. Poddeck reads the transcript of your download for sponsor language: "brought to you by", promo codes, web addresses, "after the break", plus the music around them.
    2. Ads inserted into podcasts differ from download to download, so it checks your copy, not someone else's list.
    3. Wrong? Tap Play ad to hear it or Not an ad to correct it. Poddeck remembers.
    4. Optional: "Find ads while listening" checks the next few minutes as you go, without a full transcript. It's off by default because it uses battery.
    Skip automatically: OffFind while listening: Off
    What it isn't

    Not a blocklist, not a server

    There's no shared database of ads and nobody else sees what you skip. It's pattern spotting on your own phone, which means it can miss one or catch a genuine bit of the show. That's why every break can be played or corrected.

    Fair to shows

    The ad still downloads

    The show's audio arrives exactly as published and counts as a normal download for the show. Poddeck just lets you choose whether to hear the ad.

    04 · The player

    Listening tools that stay out of the way

    Four keys under the play button, each one plain about what it does.

    Speed · 1.0–2.0×

    Speed

    One speed for everything, or a speed per show (the key then says SHOW). The ENDS time under the progress bar is worked out at your speed.

    Trim · off by default

    Trim silences

    Speeds through pauses longer than about a third of a second. Nothing is cut out; the gaps just go by faster. Stats tell you the time it saved.

    Boost · off by default

    Boost voices

    Evens out loud and quiet voices so you can hear them in the car or on a train, without riding the volume.

    Sleep · 5–60 min or episode end

    Sleep timer

    Fades out over the last 10 seconds. Shake the phone for 5 more minutes. An optional bedtime mode sets it for you after 10pm.

    Chapters · from the show or made on the phone

    Chapters

    Uses the show's own chapters and artwork. If there are none, your iPhone makes them from the transcript, with titles from Apple's on-device model.

    Per show

    Skip intros & outros

    Set seconds to skip at the start and end of a show. Skip buttons are 5–60 seconds each way; touch and hold one to change it on the spot.

    05 · New episodes

    New episodes arrive by themselves

    Ring the bell on a show and you get an alert that says where the episode went: "New from Tidewater Weekly, added to the top of Up Next". Episodes from shows set to Top or Bottom also download, ready to play.

    How it works
    1. Your iPhone checks your shows in the background every 1, 3 or 6 hours (iOS picks the exact moment).
    2. For faster alerts, a small open-source job checks your feeds on GitHub's free computers and sends a push through Apple. Your list of shows is encrypted so only that job can read it, and its logs never print show names or links.
    3. Downloads use Wi-Fi only unless you say otherwise, and played episodes are deleted straight away (or after 24 hours, if you prefer).
    Bell per showWi-Fi only: OnKeep downloads under: 2–20 GB
    Honest timing

    Alerts come "within an hour or so", not to the second. Free scheduling isn't instant, and iOS sometimes holds back background work in Low Power Mode. We'd rather tell you than promise a stopwatch.

    06 · Your library is yours

    Sync to your iCloud. Leave any time.

    Your shows, Up Next, progress, bookmarks and settings sync to your own iCloud, so a new iPhone picks up where you left off. Poddeck has no account because it doesn't need one.

    How it works
    1. Data is stored in your private iCloud space, which only you can read. It uses a small slice of your iCloud storage.
    2. Bring shows in from Pocket Casts, Overcast, Castro and others with an OPML file. Apple Podcasts links work in Add a show.
    3. Export every show as an OPML file whenever you like. No lock-in.
    4. Private feeds from Patreon, Supercast, Substack and similar work by pasting the feed link.

    Fair questions

    So how is it free?

    Because it costs nothing to run. There are no servers to pay for: your iPhone does the transcribing and ad spotting, your iCloud holds your library, and the alerts job runs on free public computing. Poddeck is a personal project, not a business, so there's no one to pay back.

    Will it ever start charging?

    There's nothing to start charging for. If that ever changed, you'd be told plainly first, and your shows can be exported as an OPML file to any other app at any time.

    Does it use much battery?

    Normal listening uses what any player uses. Making a transcript works the phone hard while it runs, which is why it only happens when you ask, or while charging if you choose that. Before the first one, Poddeck gives an estimate from your own iPhone, and Stats shows how long each one really took.

    Does it use my iCloud storage?

    A little. Your library, queue and settings are small. Downloads and transcripts stay on the phone and are not synced.

    What do I need?

    An iPhone on iOS 26 or later. Transcripts work in the languages Apple's speech recognition supports; auto-generated chapter titles are best on iPhones with Apple Intelligence and fall back to simpler titles otherwise.

    Why TestFlight and not the App Store?

    Poddeck is in testing. TestFlight is Apple's free way to install apps that are still being built. You install the free TestFlight app, open the invite link and Poddeck installs like any other app. Test builds last 90 days and update themselves.

    Can it play video podcasts or shows that need a password?

    Not yet. Poddeck is audio-first. Private feeds work when the key is in the link (Patreon, Supercast and the like); feeds that ask for a username and password aren't supported yet.
